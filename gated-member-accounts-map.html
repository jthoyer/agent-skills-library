Gated Member Accounts Map

How someone joins the Balance Tri Club calendar once sign-in moves to email and password. They sign up, ask for access and wait. The owner gets one Telegram message, decides in the console, and the allow-list does the rest. Guards stop a pre-approval being claimed by someone else.

Nothing in the request flow grants access by itself. The only thing the write rules check is the allow-list. A request row is the record of who asked and what the owner decided, and admin_decide_request() moves the two together in one step. The guards exist because, once "Confirm email" is off, an email address proves nothing.
