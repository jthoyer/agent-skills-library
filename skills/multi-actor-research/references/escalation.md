# Escalation Protocol and Coordination Principles

---

## Three Situations Requiring Escalation

Escalate to the Project Manager rather than resolving bilaterally between actors in these three situations.

### 1. Confidence Disagreement
Researcher and Evidence Auditor cannot agree on a source grade.  
→ Project Manager decides. Decision is logged.

### 2. Unresolved Challenge
Devil's Advocate raises a challenge the Researcher cannot address with available sources.  
→ Project Manager decides whether the claim is removed, qualified, or retained with a known limitation noted. Decision is logged.

### 3. Translation Distortion
Communicator flags that a claim cannot be communicated accurately to the target audience without losing its qualification.  
→ Project Manager determines whether the output requires further evidential work or whether the qualified form is sufficient for the audience's decision-making needs.

---

## Coordination Principles

**Roles are not hierarchical — they are sequential.**  
No actor outranks another within their stage. The Project Manager holds decision rights only at impasse.

**Inference is not the enemy.**  
The goal is not to eliminate reasoning — it is to label it honestly. A well-marked inference is more useful than a suppressed one.

**The output should be as confident as the evidence warrants, and no more.**  
A finding that survives the Evidence Auditor, the Devil's Advocate, and the Subject Matter Validator is a finding worth stating plainly. One that does not should say so.

**Speed is a risk variable, not a success metric.**  
The Project Manager's job is to protect the process from deadline pressure, not to accelerate through it.

---

## Decision Log Template

When the Project Manager makes an impasse decision, record it in this format:

| # | Stage | Actors in Conflict | Issue | Decision | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Stage 1 | Researcher / Evidence Auditor | Source confidence disagreement on [source] | Rated as [grade] | [reasoning] |
| 2 | Stage 2 | Devil's Advocate / Researcher | Challenge on [claim] unresolvable | Retained as known limitation | [reasoning] |
