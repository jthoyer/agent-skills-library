# Balance Tri Club — Brand Skill

Governs creative and communication outputs for Balance Triathlon Club (balancetriclub.com.au),
a Sydney-based triathlon club founded in 2006. Every output should feel like it was made by and
for real people who train hard and then grab a coffee together.

---

## Brand Essence

**Grassroots-athletic.** Not elite, not glossy — inclusive, warm, and get-stuff-done.

### Personality
- **Motivating** — pushes people to act, without pressure
- **Cheeky** — a little wit goes a long way
- **Welcoming** — every newcomer was once a nervous first-timer
- **Unpretentious** — community over performance theatre
- **Momentum-driven** — movement and energy over polish

## Colour Palette

| Name | Role | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Deep Navy | Primary background | `#0D1B2A` |
| Action Green | Primary CTA, positive actions | `#3DAF4E` |
| Energy Yellow | Secondary headline, warmth, celebration | `#F5C518` |
| Club Blue | Tertiary accent, links, info | `#3A8FD5` |
| White | Body text on dark, primary headlines | `#FFFFFF` |
| Off-White | Subtext, secondary labels | `#E8E8E8` |

### Colour rules
- Deep Navy is always the background. Never reverse to white for primary communications.
- One dominant accent per layout section — do not compete.
- Green = go / sign up. Yellow = celebration / energy. Blue = info / secondary action.
- Never accent colours in body copy — white and off-white only.

---

## Typography

Heavy, condensed, built for impact. The hierarchy is extreme and intentional.

| Role | Style |
|---|---|
| Impact headline | All-caps, condensed, heavy weight — max 6 words |
| Secondary headline | All-caps, italic or styled, accent colour |
| Body / label | Bold sans-serif, sentence case |
| Supporting copy | Regular sans-serif — use sparingly |

### Type rules
- Headlines shout with a smile — loud but not aggressive.
- All-caps for anything that must register in under a second.
- Mix weights dramatically: ultra-heavy headline + clean mid-weight body.
- Sans-serif only. No decorative or serif fonts.
- White headlines. Accent-coloured emphasis words only. Never body copy in an accent.

---

## Layout & Composition

- **Dark canvas, always.** Deep Navy is the default background.
- **High-contrast hierarchy.** Headline dominates. Supporting content recedes.
- **Numbered action lists.** Coloured circle badges (green, blue, yellow) for steps — a signature pattern.
- **Horizontal rules** at low opacity (20–30%) separate sections cleanly.
- **Brush stroke / paint elements** work as callout backgrounds in social graphics.
- **Arrow motifs** signal momentum at the end of CTAs.
- **Left-align body content.** No centred layouts for copy blocks.

---

## Tone of Voice

### Principles
- Direct. Say the thing. No filler.
- Warm but not saccharine. Encouragement over flattery.
- Action-oriented. Every communication makes the next step obvious.
- Cheeky when the moment allows — earnest when it matters.
- Inclusive. "We" not "you." The club is a team, not a service.

### Headline formula
> **[Relatable situation or hook]? [Short, energetic directive.]**

### Examples

| ❌ Don't | ✅ Do |
|---|---|
| "We invite you to consider participating in our charity ride." | "Sign up for the Charity Ride." |
| "Members are encouraged to attend the presentation evening." | "Buy your Presentation Night tickets now." |
| "Despite the inclement weather conditions…" | "Rainy Wednesday night? Let's get these 3 things done." |

## Social Media

- **Aspect ratio:** 4:5 portrait for Instagram feed; 9:16 for Stories.
- **Background:** Always Deep Navy.
- **Headline:** Fill at least 40% of the frame with the primary message.
- **CTA:** Every post ends with a single, specific action.
- **Hashtags:** 5 or fewer.
- **Emoji:** One or two max — for warmth, not as word substitutes.

---

## Iconography

- Outline / line-draw style only. No filled, gradient, or drop-shadow icons.
- White or accent colour — matched to section.
- Triathlon-relevant preferred: bikes, swim strokes, running figures, trophies, stopwatches.
- Emoji acceptable sparingly in digital/social — not in print or formal comms.

---

## Logo Usage

- Clear space: equal to height of the "B" in "BALANCE" on all sides.
- Placement: top-left on posters/banners; centred on apparel.
- On dark backgrounds: white wordmark + full-colour glyph.
- Never stretch, recolour, or apply effects to the glyph.
- Minimum: 80px wide (digital); 20mm wide (print).

---

## What This Brand Is Not

- ❌ Elite / exclusive — not a performance brand for podium athletes
- ❌ Corporate / polished — no stock imagery, no formal language
- ❌ Cluttered / busy — energy from contrast and hierarchy, not quantity
- ❌ Pastel / soft — the palette is bold; softness dilutes the signal
- ❌ Generic fitness — no lightning bolts, neon gradients, or "hustle" culture
