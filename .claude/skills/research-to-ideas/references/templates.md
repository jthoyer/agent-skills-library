# Templates for research-to-ideas

Copy these shapes. Two runs on the same research should come out looking alike.

## Idea card

One per idea past Stage 5, in ranked order.

```
### <rank>. <Verb-first name>                               <High | Medium | Low> · <score>/35
Job:          when <situation>, I want to <motivation>, so I can <outcome>
Evidence:     <idea grade> (weakest load-bearing claim: <claim>) · <n> independent sources ·
              classes: <list> · audit: <agreed | lowered from X | embodied>
Denominator:  <n tried, n succeeded, source> | estimate: <figure, basis> | Unknown
Numbers:      cost AUD <x> to first signal · <n> weeks to signal ·
              range <low> / <base> / <high> <unit> (<sources>)
Confidence:   <tier> because <the check that set it>. Lifts to <tier> if <one thing>.
Score:        reach <n> · cost <n> · speed <n> · fit <n> · upside <n> (×2 on <priority weights>)
Catch:        <most likely failure, with its basis> · severity <Blocks | Manageable | Cosmetic>
Worst case:   <AUD vs budget> · undo within a week: <yes | partly | no>
First step:   <under two hours, and the signal it reads>
```

## Decision block

```
Decision:        <what is being decided>
Decider:         <name>            Decide by: <date>
Recommendation:  <back idea X | back nothing yet: do research step Y>
Would reverse:   <the one assumption that, if false, flips the call>
Review date:     <date the Stage 8 test reports>
```

## Test plan (Stage 8)

```
Hypothesis:  We believe <who> will <do what> because <evidence, grade>.
             We will know when <measurable signal>.
Test:        <cheapest test inside the limits> · metric: <one metric> · spend cap AUD <x>
Rule:        Stop < <a> · Change <a> to under <b> · Continue <b>+
Review:      <date>
Day 1:       <actions>
Checkpoints: week 1 <…> · week 2 <…> · week 3 <…> · week 4 <…>
Risks:       <from the catch and worst case>
```

## Worked example (abridged)

Scope: decide which side income to test first. Decider: Justin, by 5 Oct. Limits: AUD 150,
4 hours a week. Priority weights: speed and cost. Coverage: forums Reached, verified revenue
Not reached, so every idea caps at Medium. Depth: Full, dispatched.

One cluster, "people pay to skip research they can't judge" (7 independent sources), frames
the job: *when I'm about to buy something complex, I want someone credible to have checked the
options, so I can decide without learning the field.* Push: "every review site is an affiliate
list" (S3). Anxiety: "how do I know the checker isn't paid either?" (S5).

Two ideas from that job:

- **Sell a paid comparison guide.** Load-bearing claim: "buyers pay AUD 10–30 for an
  independent guide." Four forum accounts agree, from one class, no proof, so it grades
  Inferred. Two of the four were guide sellers, so they drop to Asserted, leaving two
  independent accounts: still Inferred. Denominator Unknown. Confidence **Low**: only two
  independent sources, and the denominator is Unknown.
- **Run one paid research audit for a single buyer.** Load-bearing claim: "people post asking
  for exactly this and offer to pay." Five independent requests from two classes, one with a
  screenshot of a paid job: Confirmed. Denominator estimated (9 of 30 visible requests got a
  paid reply). Confidence **Medium**: coverage cap and estimated denominator.

The ranking rule puts the audit first, whatever the guide scores. The challenger's catch for the
audit: "buyers want a named expert, not a generalist" (failure story S9), severity Manageable.
Decision: back the audit. Test: offer three audits at AUD 40 in two forums. Rule: Stop < 1 paid
· Change 1 to under 3 · Continue 3+. Review 19 Oct. Could not verify: any platform-verified
revenue for either idea.
