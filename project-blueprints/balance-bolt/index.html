Three things that hold it together

  • The call is the whole game. Speed does not matter. Everyone is ranked by the gap between actual and predicted time, over or under.
  • Waves freeze at the first gun. Until then the field regroups on every sign-up. After it, only the measured time can change.
  • A time is never lost. Starts and finishes are stamped on the device, and an outbox holds them until the signal returns.

Gaps worth a look

  • The sign-up page and the timing page do not link to each other, so only admin.html connects them.
  • Two phones nudging a wave at once means the last write wins.
  • Device clocks must agree, or finish times drift by seconds.
  • Name matching is the only identity across the series, so a typo splits a racer.
