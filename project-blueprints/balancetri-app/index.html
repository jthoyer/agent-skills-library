Three things that hold it together

  • The allow-list is the only door. A request never grants anything by itself. Approval adds the address to the allow-list, and every save checks it.
  • Every commitment has an owner. All commitments were cleared on 22 October 2026, and each new one is stamped with whoever made it. Only that member, or an admin with two-factor, can change it or its result.
  • Race day is Sydney time. Pick your races starts after today, and results open on the race date, both by the club's clock and not the phone's.

Gaps worth a look

  • Nothing is emailed to members, so a mistyped address is never caught by the member.
  • The race list on the calendar still uses the phone's date, not Sydney's.
  • Admin Undo covers commitments and races, not results.
  • "Shown once" and "Later" are remembered per device, so a second device can show them again.
