From a search to the next race

How the race-day tool suite should work, from a first search to the next race. It is a plan: nothing is built yet. Each stage says which build brings it. The Measure lane says what to count, so the weekly review can pick the next change. Sources: the Notion page “Strategy: Race-day tool suite” and its four tool pages (26 October 2026), and the three design options (2 November 2026).

The plan: four builds, two gates

  1. Build 1 · weeks 1–3

    Free fuel tool inside the guide, the top-tube card, “Email me this plan”, and 10 fuelling guides.

    Cards: RD-01 to RD-03, RD-07-A2

  2. Gate 1 · week 11

    ~1,000 monthly visitors and ~100 email sign-ups. Missed? Change the angle before changing the product.

    Cards: RD-01-M2, RD-03-M1

  3. Build 2

    Gut-training ladder and payments: Lifetime and Annual.

    Cards: RD-04

  4. Build 3

    Pacing, then Countdown as the free sign-up tool, then the offline race-day view.

    Cards: RD-05 to RD-07

  5. Gate 2

    Paid conversion at or above ~3% of tool users. Missed? Fix the Unlock stage before adding tools.

    Cards: RD-04-M1

  6. Build 4

    Debrief and the annual plan, which close the loop.

    Cards: RD-08, RD-09

The weekly review

One hour a week, from the time budget. This is how the blueprint stays the guide.

  1. Read the Measure lane for every live stage.
  2. Pick the one stage furthest from its target.
  3. Name the card to change by its key, such as RD-02-A1.
  4. Make one change. Log the date, key, change and expected effect in NOTES.md.
  5. Next week, check that effect before you pick the next change.

Three things that hold it together

  • Enter details once. Every tool after the first fills itself in.
  • One loop. Pace sets the splits, the splits set the fuel, and the debrief changes the next race.
  • On the device. No accounts and no health data held. Near-zero running costs.

Open decisions

  • D1 Design direction: decided 2 November 2026. Option A’s race plan, with C’s race card as what every step writes to and what prints. RD-02-A4 and RD-02-W3 cover A’s weak spot, the guide visitor.
  • D2 Restoring a purchase with no accounts. RD-04-A2, RD-04-W1.
  • D3 Counting without tracking. Analytics that give the Measure lane its numbers without collecting health data.
  • D4 Tools to choose: email, offline copy, hosting. RD-03-S1, RD-07-S1.
  • D5 Name, and fit with TRI Momentum: its wedge, or a separate business.
  • D6 The paywall in pace set-up. Pacing is a Lifetime tool. Show a free user the finish range and lock the leg targets, or lock the whole step? RP-05-W3, RP-06-W1.
