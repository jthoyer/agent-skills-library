## Review findings (embodied mc-reviewer)
- R1 High: two halves deploy separately. Pages goes live on merge, and the Apps Script has to be pasted in by hand. If the front end ships first, `setWaveOverride` is dropped as an unknown action and the next poll wipes the pin. The move looks like it worked, then reverts. Mitigated by docs only.
- R2 Medium: a phone that fires the gun before polling a pin overwrites it. Fixed server-side.
- R3 Low: `addMissingColumns_` relies on `getLastColumn`. A stray column H on the sheet would collide.
- R4 Low: a pre-lock pin is to a wave number. Auto groups renumber around it.
