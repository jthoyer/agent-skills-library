## Findings, most costly first

**1. Structural: README contradicts itself on who sets `app.entries_link_ok`.**
- README.md:40 says the setting is one "that only those two functions set" (link and unlink).
- `20261022195352_…_5_admin_undo.sql`:62 now sets it too.
- README.md:32 points to that paragraph ("as below") for the undo case, so the reader lands on the statement it contradicts. The `entries_set_created_by` exemption is security-relevant, so a wrong list of who can set it misleads the next audit.
- Fix: in README:40, change it to "that only `admin_link_entries`, `admin_unlink_entries` and `admin_undo`'s re-insert set".

**2. Structural: the old single migration name is still cited.**
- `app.js`:214 and `app.js`:669 say `restrict_entry_writes_to_owners`. So does `scripts/check-app-ui.mjs`:2063, which is outside the listed files.
- No file by that name exists; only the five `restrict_entry_writes_1`…`_5` files do. A reader grepping for it finds nothing.
- Fix: cite `restrict_entry_writes_2` at app.js:214 and `restrict_entry_writes_3` at app.js:669, or the `_1` to `_5` range.

**3. Structural: "every entry has an owner" is stated as permanent, and the list of how an entry loses its owner is incomplete.**
- README.md:36 says "so every entry now has one", then "An entry with no owner (typed from the dashboard)".
- docs/permissions.md:65 says "so that every entry has an owner".
- But entries also lose their owner through Unlink and when a member's account is deleted (README:87, README:103, migration 5:31). Neither line mentions those.
- admin.js:1485 ("predate attribution or came from the dashboard") has the same gap, and so does README:87.
- Fix: say "every entry had an owner on 22 October 2026". Then list all the ways an entry ends up unowned: dashboard, Unlink, account deleted.

**4. Typo-level: permissions.md:31 says results are only for a "past" race.**
- The code allows results on race day itself: `r.date <=` Sydney today (migration 3:18).
- README:33 and guides/index.html:650 both say "race day".
- Fix: change "past" to "today's or past".

**5. Typo-level: permissions.md:76 says undoing a deleted entry always keeps its owner.**
- It leaves out the case where the owner's account is gone. Then the entry comes back unowned (migration 5:57–59, README:103).
- Fix: add "or unowned if that account is gone".

**6. Typo-level: guides/index.html:793 explains a name clash as "typed in before you signed up".**
- Every commitment was cleared on 22 October, and inserts must now carry your own name. So a clash now comes from a dashboard entry, an unlinked entry or a deleted account.
- Fix: change it to "It may have been added by the club admin, or left after an account was removed."
