## The view that drove the change

Both skills are strong on rigour, weak on business stakes. Research started from evidence, not
from the decision it serves. Strategy mapped stakeholder reactions, not what each party is
measured on. One rule governs both: the business hat shapes the question and the translation,
never the grade.

## Changes

- Research: Decision Hook (PM, before grading) at the top of the Stage 1 Register; Evidence
  Auditor grades on evidence alone; Subject Matter Validator tests relevance to the hook;
  Communicator leads with the effect on the decision or metric.
- Strategy: Problem Framer writes or imports the hook; Stakeholder Proxy adds "measured on";
  Decision Architect states one criterion as a business metric (checker rule R9, with
  `skipped.metric` as the escape); the Conviction Bet names the metric.
