# Ridgeway Tri Club — Membership Policy (approved 2026-09-01)

## 1. Fees
The annual membership fee is $180. Juniors (under 18) pay $90.
Members who renew before 31 October get a 15% early-bird discount.

## 2. Refunds
A new member may cancel within 14 days of joining for a full refund.
After 14 days, fees are not refundable.

## 3. Juniors
Members under 18 must have a parent or guardian sign the membership form.
Junior forms cannot be completed online; they are signed at the club desk.
