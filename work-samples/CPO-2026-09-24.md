## CPO's full reply (verbatim — the entire work sample)

Verdict: this is a well-scoped UI design task with one discipline. No
pipeline is needed, and neither is a ux-designer pass: the orchestrating
session can build the concepts directly. ux-designer only becomes worth it
once an option is picked and cody needs a build spec.

Brand notes from the repo:
- The club blue is already in `styles.css:10` as `--brand-blue: #00a3e0`.
  Use it, not a colour sampled from the logo.
- Navy `--ink: #182542` is the text and contrast colour. `--yellow: #f8c44f`
  is for accents only.
- Headings use Outfit 600/700 and body text uses DM Sans.
- The logo is at `/home/user/BalanceTRI-app/assets/balance-logo.png`.

Concepts:
1. **Full-bleed blue hero.** White logo, a short tagline, a white "Sign In"
   button and a ghost "Just Browsing" button.
2. **Split layout.** A photo in the top half, a white card below with the
   logo and a blue "Sign In" button.
3. **Minimal.** Light background, centred logo, a thin blue swim/bike/run
   line motif, and "Just Browsing" as a text link.

In all three, "Sign In" is the main button and each button is at least 44px
tall. Keep the tone warm and community-first.

I couldn't delegate here (this session has no Agent tool), so I did this

## Ground truth, verified independently against the repo (not from CPO's narration)

- `styles.css` root tokens, read directly: `--ink: #182542`, `--muted:
  #566177`, `--line: #d9dfe8`, `--canvas: #f7f8fa`, `--yellow: #f8c44f`,
  `--green: #116b43`, `--green-bg: #e8f6ee`, `--navy-bg: #eef2fb`,
  `--brand-blue: #00a3e0`. CPO's quoted values and the `styles.css:10`
  citation for `--brand-blue` were accurate.
- `styles.css:108-109`: `.primary-button { background: var(--yellow);
  color: var(--ink); }`. CPO's claim that `--yellow` "is for accents only"
  is false — it's the fill for every primary button in the app, ink text on
  top. This is a real defect in a brand-fidelity claim, not a stale-cache or
  ambiguity issue: the token's *value* was quoted correctly, its *role* was
  not, and nothing in the brief asked CPO to guess at usage when it could
  grep for it.
- Contrast, computed independently: white text (`#ffffff`) on
  `--brand-blue: #00a3e0` is ≈2.87:1 — under the 3:1 floor for large text
  and far under 4.5:1 for body text. Concept 1 ("White logo... a white
  'Sign In' button") proposed exactly this pairing. Dark ink (`#182542`) on
  the same blue is ≈5.3–7.3:1, comfortably passing. The orchestrating
  session caught this itself later (independently, computing the same
  ratios) and used dark ink text on blue throughout the design that
  actually shipped.
- None of CPO's three concepts were used verbatim downstream. The
  orchestrating session built its own three concepts, iterated through
  several more rounds against direct user feedback, and landed on a design
  ("Blue Card Sheet") that shares only the blue-background premise with
  CPO's brief.
- CPO's probe report (no `Agent`/`Task` tool; embodying/self-scoping
  instead of delegating) checked out — confirmed correct against the
  session's actual tool list.
- Word count: CPO's reply is ≈208 words against a stated 150-word budget,
  spent mostly on three concepts the brief said would not be used
  verbatim, rather than on the repo facts and constraints that were.
